About Silverton

Silverton is a local auto care partner focused on dependable tire service, oil changes, brake repairs, and general maintenance for drivers who want honest advice and quality care.

Our Story

Silverton was built around a simple idea: every driver deserves clear guidance, fair service, and dependable repairs. From routine maintenance to larger repair needs, the goal is to make the experience straightforward and stress-free.

We proudly serve the Toms River area and work hard to keep vehicles safe, reliable, and ready for the road.

What We Offer

  • Tire service including tire sales, rotations, and replacements
  • Oil changes and routine maintenance
  • Brake repairs and safety inspections
  • General auto care for everyday vehicle needs

Why Drivers Choose Silverton

  • Dependable service that keeps your vehicle running smoothly
  • Honest advice with clear recommendations and transparent pricing
  • Quality care from a team that treats every visit with care
  • Local focus with service built around the community
