About

With over 50 years of industry expertise, Silverton Motors stands as a beacon of automotive excellence. Our journey has been paved with strong relationships forged with both manufacturers and dealerships, allowing us to bring customers unparalleled discounts and exceptional value.

About Us

Enter Silverton Motors, where simplicity and satisfaction come together for residents seeking new or pre-owned vehicles. We have transformed the car-buying experience by making it easier, more transparent, and far more enjoyable.

Here is how we do it:

  • Passion for Cars: Our team lives and breathes automobiles. Whether you are looking for a sleek sedan, a rugged SUV, or an eco-friendly hybrid, we understand that your vehicle is more than transportation—it is part of your lifestyle.
  • Committed Sales Staff: We are not here to pressure you into a hasty decision. Our sales professionals take the time to understand your needs, preferences, and budget, offering genuine guidance every step of the way.
  • Unbeatable Customer Experience: From the moment you walk into our showroom to the test drive and final paperwork, we focus on making your experience smooth, welcoming, and stress-free.

Contact Us

Whether you are shopping for a new vehicle, a pre-owned option, or simply want expert advice, Silverton Motors is here to help. Reach out to our team for guidance on purchasing, financing, leasing, trade-ins, and more.
